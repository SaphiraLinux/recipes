# Commercial licensing for Saphira-owned software

Saphira-owned packages in this collection are licensed under the
Business Source License 1.1 (`BUSL-1.1`), each with the Saphira
Additional Use Grant, a Change Date of 2030-09-06 (four years after
first public release), and GPL-2.0-or-later as the Change License.
Four-year-old Saphira code therefore becomes genuinely open source;
every new release carries its own clock.

The public message is simple:

> Saphira is free — including commercial use. What is not free is
> taking Saphira-owned code out of Saphira and commercialising it as
> somebody else's product.

In matrix form, under the Additional Use Grant:

| Use | Grant |
|-----|-------|
| Business runs Saphira | FREE |
| Hosting company runs Saphira | FREE |
| Sell hosting/services running on Saphira | FREE |
| Make money using Saphira | FREE |
| Modify Saphira for your own deployment | FREE |
| Lift Maildragon into another paid product | NOT GRANTED |
| Put our healthchecks in another appliance | NOT GRANTED |
| Take saphira-network into another distro | NOT GRANTED |
| Turn our tooling into somebody else's SaaS | NOT GRANTED |

Uses marked NOT GRANTED require a separate commercial licence from
AKADATA LIMITED, the copyright holder. To enquire about commercial
licensing, contact AKADATA LIMITED via the channels published at
<https://saphira.vm2.uk/>.

No pricing, terms, or contractual commitments are stated here; they
are agreed directly between AKADATA LIMITED and the licensee.

Previously published releases that were issued under other licences
(such as MIT) remain under those licences. New releases carry the
licence stated in each package's metadata and shipped licence text.
